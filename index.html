In the STS department, I’m a research assistant to Dr. Jens Pohlmann. We were awarded a 2027 UC VOICE grant to continue our work on campus & platform speech. Dr. Finn Brunton is supervising my forthcoming senior thesis on prediction markets.

I wrote opinion columns for The California Aggie. My favorites are about prediction market regulation, the gamification of U.S. military operations, seed oils, and t-shirts.
