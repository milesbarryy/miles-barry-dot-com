Hello! My name is Miles Barry. I’m an undergraduate student at UC Davis, majoring in Applied Statistics and Science & Technology Studies (STS). I’m currently abroad at Fudan University in Shanghai.

I’m working on Cosine, a platform allowing people to discover, and bet on undiscovered musicians.

I’m the associate director of ASUCD’s Innovation and Research Lab (IRL), where I’ve led projects including the AI Use Survey Study and the UC Davis 2025 basic needs report.

From June through September 2026, I interned at my hometown paper The Point Reyes Light. I wrote pieces like “Another bad year for seal pups,” “Creek swimming holes get poor marks for cleanliness,” “On July 4 in Woodacre, a valley reunites,” and “Church discussion asks, When is war just?”

In Davis’s STS department, I’m a research assistant to Dr. Jens Pohlmann. We were awarded a 2027 UC VOICE grant to continue analysing student views on campus & platform speech. Dr. Finn Brunton is supervising my senior thesis on prediction markets.

I used to write opinion columns for The California Aggie. My favorite pieces are about prediction market regulation, the gamification of U.S. military operations, seed oils, and T-shirts.

From Jan. 2024 to January 2026, I led video production at the Student Startup Center, where I directed and edited shorts about our programs, including Innovation for Impact, Startup School, and Build Black. I also interviewed founders during our speaker series, taught workshops on video production and Figma, and provided creative support to student founders.

During my gap year, I interned at the Bronx Defenders.
